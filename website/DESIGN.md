# Website surface

Mode: Persuade. Extend Wordvale's established cream, sage, and deep green identity onto a public product introduction. Keep the app's design system authoritative for app UI; these choices apply to the website only.

Use an editorial two-column hero, a working illustrative product preview, an open three-part recording-to-transcript sequence, an immersive theme section, readable feature disclosures, and a direct download finish. Manrope carries the headings; DM Sans carries body and controls; italic serif phrases provide a softer counterpoint. Fonts are bundled locally with their licenses.

The flowing continuous Wordvale mark is the signature motion. It warps through the recording demo rather than scaling as a single image. Theme changes transition the whole page. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Mobile stacks the hero and changes the workflow into compact visual/text rows.

Product facts come from the repository. No testimonials, usage statistics, or guarantees. Keep alpha status visible. The demo is labeled illustrative and never captures visitor audio. Every control must perform its named action.

Use cases use a compact, non-sticky tabbed showcase in this order: lectures, meetings, voice recording, video subtitles. Pair each with an illustrative visual and short practical copy. Tabs support arrow keys, Home, and End. Avoid a long text list or sticky headings that can collide with later content.
